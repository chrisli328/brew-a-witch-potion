# Brew a Witch Potion (web demo build)

A cosy witch game you play in the browser, held sideways on a phone or on a desktop. Grow herbs on the real clock, brew potions in the cauldron with Kuro the cat, sell them to the village, and forage berries and bugs in Whisperwood.

Play: https://chrisli328.github.io/brew-a-witch-potion/

Controls: WASD or arrow keys to walk, E or Space to use, B for the bag, R for recipes. On a phone, drag on the left half of the screen to walk and tap the round button to use.

This is a concept demo, not a released game. Villagers, buyers and other names are simulated. Time follows your real clock, so plants keep growing while you are away. Your progress is saved in this browser only.
